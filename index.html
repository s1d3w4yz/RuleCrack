Rule Chain Lab

A mask is just a chain of small transformation steps — capitalize, substitute, append — applied to a word in order. Build a chain below, watch each step fire one at a time, and see what happens when you reorder, add, or remove a step. The same chain exports as a Hashcat rule.

Steps run top to bottom. Drag isn't needed — use the arrows to move a step and watch the trace change.

Order changes the result. Substituting before capitalizing can skip a letter that capitalizing would otherwise touch, and prepending before appending builds a different string than the reverse. Move a step with the arrows and compare the trace on the right — no two orderings are guaranteed to produce the same output.
